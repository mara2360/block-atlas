Architecture

A public settlement ledger

Thousands of independently operated nodes verify the same transaction history. Miners compete to add blocks while nodes enforce the rules and reject invalid changes.

Monetary policy

Predictable issuance

New bitcoin enters circulation through block rewards that halve roughly every four years. The maximum supply is enforced by network consensus, not a company’s promise.

Primary use

Durable global value transfer

Bitcoin prioritizes censorship resistance, final settlement, and long-term monetary credibility. The Lightning Network can support smaller, faster payments above the base layer.

Tradeoffs

Security over flexibility

  • Proof of work consumes significant energy.
  • Base-layer throughput is intentionally limited.
  • Self-custody carries irreversible responsibility.
  • Scripting is less expressive than general smart-contract networks.
Research questions

What to verify

  • Miner economics and fee-market health
  • Custody choices and key management
  • Network decentralization
  • The assumptions behind any yield or wrapped-Bitcoin product
