Research without
the rush.

A repeatable way to separate durable signals from urgency, tribal narratives, and price-driven certainty.

Understand the why

Identify the problem a network says it solves and who benefits. Decide whether a decentralized system is actually useful, before you look at price.

Ask yourself

  • What problem does it claim to solve, in one sentence?
  • Who is the user, and who profits if it grows?
  • Would an ordinary database do the same job?
Red flag

The only use case anyone can name is “the price will go up.”

Field tip

Write your thesis down before you open a chart. If you can’t, you’re not ready.

Map the risk

Examine custody, code, liquidity, governance and incentive risk. Every convenience carries a tradeoff worth naming.

Ask yourself

  • Custody: who actually holds the keys?
  • Code: has it been audited, or hacked before?
  • Liquidity: could you exit if you needed to?
  • Governance: who can change the rules, and how?
Red flag

High yields with no clear explanation of where the money comes from.

Field tip

Name the worst realistic outcome, then size your position as if it will happen.

Check the signal

Rely on primary documentation and transparent data. Observe patiently instead of trusting screenshots, slogans or anonymous certainty.

Ask yourself

  • Is there official documentation and open code?
  • Can the key claims be verified on-chain?
  • Who disagrees with the thesis, and why?
Red flag

Screenshots, anonymous “insiders” and countdown timers telling you to hurry.

Field tip

Wait a week. Durable signals survive patience; hype usually doesn’t.
